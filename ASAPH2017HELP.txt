🪐 ASAPH SERVERS 2017 — INITIALIZATION & CONNECTIVITY MANIFEST 🪐
================================================================================

This document details the precise initialization routines and target redirection 
parameters required to connect your legacy mid-2017 game client.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LOCAL COMPILER INITIALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before booting the game executable, your PC needs to spin up the server ports.

1. Unzip this full folder structure onto your PC Desktop or Drive.
2. Ensure you have the Bun engine installed on Windows via PowerShell:
   powershell -c "irm bun.sh/install.ps1 | iex"
3. Double-click the 'start-server.bat' file inside this root folder.

The batch script will automatically handle:
  - Checking your system environment variables.
  - Downloading any missing dependencies into your 'node_modules' tree.
  - Launching your primary server infrastructure hooks.

Confirm your black console window outputs show these two confirmation logs:
  - "🚀 API Infrastructure listening on port 3000"
  - "🌐 Photon Multiplayer Server actively listening on port 8080"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GAME CLIENT SHORTCUT OVERRIDES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Legacy Unity clients must be explicitly instructed to target your localhost 
sub-systems instead of official defunct game servers. 

Follow these steps exactly to connect:
1. Locate your 2017 'RecRoom.exe' file inside your game build directory.
2. Right-click 'RecRoom.exe' and choose "Create Shortcut".
3. Right-click the new shortcut icon on your desktop and click "Properties".
4. Find the "Target" field box. Go to the very end of the existing path string, 
   press Space once, and append these exact parameters:
   -api=http://localhost:3000 -photon=localhost:8080

5. Click Apply, then click OK.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FORCING PCVR HEADSET LAUNCH PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If the game shortcut boots strictly on your flat screen monitor instead of 
engaging your SteamVR or Meta Quest Link tracking runtime, add the native VR flag.

Append '-vr' directly onto the end of your shortcut Target redirection string:
  -api=http://localhost:3000 -photon=localhost:8080 -vr

*Important Note: Put your headset on and verify tracking boundaries are fully 
active BEFORE double-clicking your desktop shortcut, or your avatar hands 
will get permanently locked into the floor grid.
